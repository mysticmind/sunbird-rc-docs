# Possibilities

Ideas for what you can build with Sunbird RC across education, healthcare, government and employment.

<figure><img src="/images/possibilities-e4fd85e3.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Sunbird Registry and Credentials provides a secure, scalable way to manage digital identities and credentials. Here are some of the areas where it can be used:

:::: tabs
== 🎓 Education

Manage educational credentials such as degrees, certificates and diplomas.

* Institutions can securely issue and verify digital credentials.
* Students get a secure, portable way to share their achievements.

== 🏥 Healthcare

Manage healthcare credentials such as medical licenses, certifications and training records.

* Providers can ensure their staff are qualified and competent.
* Patients can verify the credentials of their healthcare providers.

== 🏛️ Government

Manage citizen identities and credentials such as driver's licenses, passports and voter registration.

* Improves the efficiency and accuracy of government services.
* Enhances the security and privacy of citizen data.

== 💼 Employment

Manage employment credentials such as job certifications, training records and performance reviews.

* Employers can ensure their staff are qualified and competent.
* Employees can securely share their credentials with potential employers.
::::
